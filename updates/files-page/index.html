Customer upload links and a Files page

Send a customer upload link from Help Scout or AirShop. Customers drop photos, video, and CAD. Then browse every file on Inquiries → Files: preview, download, or open in Fusion.

Customers send files from a phone, an inquiry form, or email. Until now those files were scattered across sidebar thumbs and individual inquiries. Two pieces now cover the whole path: a customer upload page, and a Files page that lists everything your shop has received.

A link they can actually use

Create a link from three places:

  • Help Scout: open a conversation and copy Customer upload link (or Link & QR) from the AirShop sidebar.
  • Inquiry drawer: open an inquiry in AirShop and expand Customer upload link.
  • Customer record: open a customer under Inquiries → Customers and click Upload link.

Paste the link into a reply, text, or email. The customer opens /u/..., drops files, and sees a confirmation on the same page. No AirShop account. Up to 50 MB per file: photos, video, PDF, and common CAD. When an inquiry is tied to Help Scout, the sidebar and inquiry drawer reuse the same URL until it expires or you reset it.

Upload thumbs still appear in the Help Scout sidebar when you use Help Scout. When a file finishes, AirShop can post an internal note with a staff view link. While you are signed in, new uploads also trigger an in-app notification you can click to open the inquiry.

Then find every file in one place

Open Inquiries → Files (also under the main nav: Inquiries → Files) to see customer upload links, inquiry form attachments, and staff uploads in one searchable grid: who sent it, what it links to, and when it arrived. Customer uploads also list under Attachments on the linked inquiry.

The detail drawer shows a thumbnail for photos and 3D models, including isometric previews for STEP and STL. From there you can view full-screen, download a copy, or Open in Fusion for STEP, IGES, and STL. Source, customer, ticket, and inquiry links sit below the preview.

Staff view links now land on /files instead of the old /uploads path. Bookmarks and note links redirect automatically.
