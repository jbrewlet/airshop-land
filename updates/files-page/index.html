Every customer file in one place

Customer uploads, inquiry attachments, and Help Scout files now live on one Files page, with previews, downloads, Fusion launch, and links back to the ticket or inquiry they belong to.

Photos from a upload link. STEP files on an inquiry form. CAD your team added in the thread. Until now, those files were scattered across Help Scout, individual inquiries, and sidebar thumbs. Files brings them together.

Open Inquiries → Files to see every file your shop has received in one searchable grid: who sent it, what it links to, and when it arrived.

One grid for every upload path

  • Customer upload links: files sent through the Help Scout sidebar or any `/u/...` link you share with customers.
  • Inquiry form attachments: CAD and documents customers attach when they submit a form.
  • Staff inquiry uploads: files your team adds on an inquiry's Attachments section.

Filter by type, search by filename or customer, and sort by date or size. No more opening five conversations to find last week's STEP file.

Preview, download, and open in Fusion

The detail drawer shows a thumbnail for photos and 3D models, including isometric previews for STEP and STL. From there you can:

  • View the file full-screen in AirShop's integrated viewer.
  • Download a copy to your machine.
  • Open in Fusion for STEP, IGES, and STL. Two clicks from grid to CAD, no manual download step.

Source, customer, ticket, and inquiry links sit right below the preview so context stays attached to the file.

Help Scout still works the same way

Upload thumbs still appear in the Help Scout sidebar. Internal notes still post when a customer upload finishes. Staff view links now land on /files instead of the old /uploads path. Bookmarks and note links redirect automatically.
