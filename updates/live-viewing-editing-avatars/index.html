See who's viewing or editing with you

User icons now show which teammates are on the same record right now, and whether they're viewing or editing, so nobody quietly overwrites someone else's work.

Two people opening the same inventory item, quote, or customer at once used to be invisible. Whoever saved last won, and the other person found out the hard way. Now, whenever a teammate is on the same record, you'll see their initials appear as a user icon right on the page.

Viewing vs. editing, at a glance

User icons stack in the header of the record, most recent on top. Hover one to see the teammate's name and what they're doing. A small edit badge marks anyone actively making changes, so you know at a glance whether it's safe to jump in or better to wait and check with them first.

Where it shows up

  • Inventory items and locations
  • Sources
  • Customers
  • Inquiries
  • Quotes
  • Stocktakes

No setup required

There's nothing to turn on. User icons only appear when someone else is actually there. The rest of the time, a record looks exactly like it always has. They clear themselves out within seconds of a teammate navigating away or closing the tab.
